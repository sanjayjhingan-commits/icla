G. Seminar & Research Project

Optional — 3 credits
Optional
Academic Seminar and the Graduation Research Project (GRP) are advanced research courses taken in Years 3–4. They're not required for graduation but look great on a resumé and deepen your expertise. To be eligible you need 3 years completed, all Foundation courses done, a GPA of at least 2.5, and advisor approval.
